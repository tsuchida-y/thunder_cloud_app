## 説明文

Thunderhead Watch tells you when a thunderhead (a tall, towering cloud) is visible in the sky from where you are, and in which direction and how high to look.

Open the home screen and see, at a glance, the direction and distance of the thunderheads you can see right now. Tap "Find the cloud" and point your phone: the app guides you with prompts like "Turn right 35°" and gives a light vibration when you are facing it. Height is shown in fists at arm's length, so you know how far up to look.

Notifications are sent once per cloud, at least 30 minutes apart, up to 3 times a day. No notifications are sent between dusk and dawn, when thunderheads cannot be seen.

Japan only: the data covers Japan, so places outside Japan cannot be watched.

PLUS (paid)
- Extend the watch range to 90 km (free: 30 km)
- Watch up to 3 places, such as home or school, in addition to where you are
- Home screen widget
Plus is available as a monthly subscription (with a 7-day free trial for first-time subscribers) or a one-time purchase. Prices are shown in your local currency in the App Store and in the app. The monthly plan renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. You can cancel anytime in your App Store settings. The one-time purchase is a single payment with no auto-renewal.

ABOUT THE DATA
Cloud positions are built from the Japan Meteorological Agency's high-resolution precipitation nowcast and lightning nowcast, which show where heavy rain and lightning are. Cloud height is estimated from Himawari infrared images, and sky conditions from AMeDAS observations. The information shown in this app is not issued by the Japan Meteorological Agency.
Source: Japan Meteorological Agency website (processed by the app)
